How to play

  • 1

    MoveArrow keys or A, D, W, S. Or drag the paddle with the mouse. On a phone, drag anywhere on the screen.

  • 2

    PowerHold the paddle and pull it back (or hold Down) to charge the bar. Power stays until you hit, so you can move forward to the ball freely. No power drops the ball just past the net. The green sweet spot is the most accurate. Too much and the ball flies out.

  • 3

    ServePress Space, or tap, when it's your serve. The dotted line shows where it will land. The serve switches every 2 points.

  • 4

    ScoreLand the ball on your opponent's half. A miss, a net, an out or a double bounce gives them the point.

  • 5

    WinFirst to 11 points, and you must win by 2.

  • 6

    ExtrasPress M to mute. Press Esc or tap the X to leave a match. Playing a friend? Send them an emote.
